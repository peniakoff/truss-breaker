Privacy in Truss Breaker

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Controller and contact

Tomasz Miller operates this website. For privacy questions, email contact@millers.dev.

Data on your device

Projects, truss geometry, loads and calculation results are stored locally in your browser. Your language choice is stored there too. The app does not send projects or results to its own server. Python and PDF exports are downloaded to your device. You can remove app data by clearing this site's browser data.

Hosting

Cloudflare serves the site's static files. It may process technical connection data, including your IP address, request time and requested resource, to deliver and secure the site. Truss Breaker has no user account or its own behavior analytics.

Purpose, basis and retention

Technical data is used to deliver and secure the site; correspondence is used to reply. The basis is the controller’s legitimate interest. I keep correspondence while handling the matter, normally no longer than 12 months after it ends, unless law or legal claims require longer. Hosting log periods depend on Cloudflare’s policies; email me for details.

Correspondence and rights

If you email me, I use your address and message to reply. An email provider handles the correspondence. You can ask for access to, correction, erasure or restriction of correspondence data, and object where processing rests on legitimate interest. You may also complain to a data protection authority. Use the email address above for requests.
